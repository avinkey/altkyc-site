🧠 About AltKYC

AltKYC is a decentralized gray-market platform for the exchange of verified KYC/KYB credentials.

💡 How It Works

  • Users choose to act as Buyers or Sellers of KYC services
  • Sellers post verified accounts for specific platforms (e.g. Bybit, OKX)
  • Buyers can browse listings or submit requests
  • All communication is off-chain and encrypted (Telegram, ProtonMail, etc.)
  • Crypto payments only – currently USDT (Binance Pay or on-chain)

🛡️ Privacy First

We do not log IPs, identities, or communication. Everything is hosted via decentralized storage (IPFS).
